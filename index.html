About PMSL

Modeling systems for better performance

A research lab focused on the performance of computing systems and communication networks.

The Performance Modeling & Simulation Lab (PMSL) studies computer-system performance, with particular attention to modern communication networks and emerging technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), 5G, blockchain and Web 3.

Performance evaluation helps people understand how systems can provide a suitable quality of service while using resources effectively. Research considers processors, operating systems, networks, knowledge-based systems, intelligent systems and databases.

How we work

A practical modeling cycle

A typical performance study describes a system, represents its behavior, then compares operating scenarios.

01 · DEFINE

Describe the system

Identify components, workloads and measures such as response time or throughput.

02 · MODEL

Represent its behavior

Use an analytical model or simulation to examine possible operating conditions.

03 · EVALUATE

Compare alternatives

Reason about quality of service, resource use and potential design improvements.
