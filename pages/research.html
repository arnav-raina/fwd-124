The approach

Why model system performance?

Performance evaluation helps compare design choices and understand how systems behave under different workloads.

01 · DEFINE

Describe the system

Identify components, workload, and measures such as response time or network throughput.

02 · MODEL

Represent its behavior

Use an analytical model or simulation to explore system behavior under different conditions.

03 · EVALUATE

Compare alternatives

Use results to reason about quality of service, resource use, and design improvements.
